Table S3. Llama 2 7B generation duration, output volume, throughput, and latency.

Gen. is total timed generation duration over the evaluation workload. Batch tokens/s is generation_tokens_per_second. Mean, p50, p95, and Single tokens/s come from the separate single-example latency benchmark. They are not alternative summaries of the batched workload. All entries are the reported means; p50 and p95 are means of run-level percentile statistics when aggregated across seeds.

The supplied generate_batch timer starts after tokenization, source features, and CRAT router computation. These timings exclude that setup work. Different output token counts also affect raw duration; tokenizer differences limit cross-backbone interpretation of tokens/s.
