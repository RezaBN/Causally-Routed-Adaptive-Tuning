Table 3. Quality ranges on parameter grids shared by all three language pairs.

Each range is the maximum minus minimum of reported means. Common values are 0.05/0.1/0.2 for KL, causal-prior, and morphology coefficients; 0.001/0.005/0.01 for sparsity; 2/4/8 for temperature; 8/16/32 for rank; 32/64/96 for head budget; and 1/2/3/4 for top k. A smaller range indicates less variation over that grid, not stronger statistical evidence of robustness.
