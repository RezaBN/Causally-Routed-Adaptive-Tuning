# Supplementary Materials

This directory is reserved for supplementary materials associated with the **Causally-Routed Adaptive Tuning (CRAT)** project.

The materials placed here will complement the main experimental results and provide additional analyses, interpretations, and supporting evidence that are useful for understanding and evaluating the proposed method.

## Planned Contents

As the project develops, this directory may include:

### Extended Analysis

Additional analyses of the main experimental results, including detailed comparisons between CRAT and the baseline methods across models, datasets, language pairs, random seeds, and experimental settings.

### Ablation Analysis and Discussion

Detailed interpretation of the CRAT ablation experiments, including the effects of removing or modifying individual components of the method and discussion of their contribution to overall performance.

### Statistical Analysis

Supplementary statistical analyses supporting the reported results, such as:

- significance tests;
- effect-size estimates;
- confidence intervals;
- seed-level variability analyses;
- pairwise method comparisons; and
- robustness and sensitivity analyses.

### Additional Tables and Figures

Extended tables, plots, and visualizations that supplement the main results reported in the manuscript, including results that may be too detailed for inclusion in the main paper.

### Qualitative Analysis

Selected qualitative examples and case studies intended to provide additional insight into model behavior, translation quality, error patterns, and differences between CRAT and comparison methods.

### Robustness and Sensitivity Analyses

Additional experiments examining the stability of CRAT under different hyperparameters, training conditions, model backbones, datasets, or other experimental choices.

### Cross-Model Analysis

Comparative analyses across different underlying language models as additional model backbones are incorporated into the project.

### Extended Discussion

Additional interpretation of the experimental findings, including possible mechanisms underlying observed results, limitations, failure cases, implications, and directions for future investigation.

### Reproducibility Notes

Supplementary information needed to reproduce or interpret specific analyses, including analysis settings, conventions, processing decisions, and other relevant methodological details.

## Planned Organization

As supplementary materials are added, this directory may evolve toward a structure such as:

```text
supplementary/
├── analysis/
├── figures/
├── tables/
├── qualitative/
├── robustness/
├── discussion/

```

The exact organization will be refined as the corresponding analyses and manuscript materials are completed.

## Current Status

This directory currently serves as a placeholder for forthcoming supplementary analyses and documentation. Experimental outputs currently available in the repository are maintained separately from these interpretive and supplementary materials.
