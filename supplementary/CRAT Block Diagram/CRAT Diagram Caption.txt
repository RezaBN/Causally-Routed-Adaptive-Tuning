CRAT architecture and training. (a) Offline Subspace-Intervened Path Patching (SIPP) on a frozen language model identifies a union of direction-specific attention heads and MLP-scored decoder layers. The selected heads receive operational source, indicator, or positional roles; discovery also produces fixed directional causal priors. (b) A prompt-conditioned router combines mean frozen prompt embeddings, trainable language embeddings, and four source-string features to produce independent sigmoid gates. Three gates scale selected query/output (Q/O) updates, while associated unique key/value (K/V) updates remain ungated. The integration gate scales low-rank residual adapters applied after complete selected decoder-layer outputs, without changing pretrained MLP weights. All original backbone parameters remain frozen. Training and validation use soft gates; generation retains the top two gate values without renormalization and holds them fixed throughout decoding. (c) Training combines target-token cross entropy, teacher-to-student KL regularization, causal-prior alignment, morphology-feature supervision, and a mean-gate activity penalty. The morphology predictor uses pooled teacher-forced target hidden states and is unused during generation. Selection counts and adapter rank denote the manuscript's reference configuration.
